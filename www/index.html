Make alarms reliable

  1. Allow notifications when asked.
  2. Phone Settings → Apps → Upkeep → Battery → Unrestricted.
  3. Notifications → Upkeep → make sure sound is on for "Stand-up & nudges".
  4. Keep your phone's alarm volume up — on Silent, reminders use it.
